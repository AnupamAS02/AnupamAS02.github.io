Before you get in touch

A first conversation can help clarify whether my experience is a fit. Scope and availability still need to be discussed. Open a scoping email, then edit the outline in your email app before sending.

  • Bring a general outline. Describe the product type, what you want to understand, your preferred timeframe, and your connection to the system owner. It’s fine if some details are still undecided.
  • Agree boundaries before testing. Any testing requires written authorisation from the system owner and an agreed scope: permitted systems and environments, methods, exclusions, timing, and a contact if testing needs to stop. An enquiry is not permission to test.
  • Keep ordinary email non-sensitive. Don’t send passwords, API keys, customer data, private target URLs, vulnerability details, or authorisation documents. First discuss an appropriate secure way to share anything sensitive.
