Web & API assessment preparation

A clearer starting point for your team.

Before requesting testing, check what your team understands and what still needs a conversation. Get practical preparation steps—not a verdict on your security.

Preparation, not permission. This is not a security score, legal advice or permission to test. Any testing requires written authorisation from the system owner and an agreed scope. Fit and availability still need to be discussed.

Your preparation checklist

Work through these questions with your team. You can select and copy this checklist for your own notes. If the interactive helper cannot load, this checklist covers the same preparation areas.

  1. Ownership and authorisation. Identify the system owner, who can give written authorisation, and any third-party permissions needed. Confirm written authorisation for the agreed scope before testing.
  2. Intended scope. Outline the goal, broad web/API areas and exclusions internally. Agree precise systems, permitted methods and third-party boundaries through an appropriate secure channel before testing.
  3. Environment. Discuss the intended environment, live data, operational risks, recovery arrangements and stop conditions. Do not assume staging is isolated or production testing is acceptable.
  4. Access. Identify relevant user roles and dedicated test-account arrangements. Agree a secure way to provision access after scope is agreed; do not email credentials.
  5. Timing and coordination. Identify a preferred window, release constraints and coordination or emergency-stop contacts. Discuss availability before agreeing a schedule.
